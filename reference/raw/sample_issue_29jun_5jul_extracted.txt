Why do delivery volumes matter? 

Rising delivery volumes for a stock indicate that investors and traders are taking delivery of 
the shares of the company. In case this happens with a rise in prices, then the trend could 
be durable. 

Similarly, falling delivery volumes show that investors and traders aren’t interested in taking 
delivery of the stock. In case the stock price rises with a fall in delivery volumes, then the 
trend might not be durable.

Wondering why FIIs and DIIs generally move in opposite directions? 

FIIs are more impacted by global events like wars, geopolitical tensions and trade wars. Their holdings in India 
are impacted due to foreign exchange volatility, and any change in monetary policy or fiscal policy by central 
banks globally. 

DIIs on the other hand are mostly concerned by what is happening in India. When you see FIIs selling shares 
and DIIs buying them, it’s because sellers will find buyers in the market.
